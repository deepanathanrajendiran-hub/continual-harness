review and fix review, controller resolution, final integrated and focused test
outputs, both entrypoint outputs, and the complete 18-control directory.  It
does not rerun, combine, or reinterpret that evidence for Task 10.

and records SHA-256.  The copied records retain historical absolute paths and
original bytes.  In particular, `task-9-integrated-suite.txt` records 530
integrated passes at `ea0770c` and `task-9-fix1-focused.txt` records 33 focused
passes at `2f65f2a`; they are distinct results and must never be summed.
